We are always interested in hearing from people who are curious about how mathematics can help tackle problems in sustainable energy. We keep the group small so that everyone gets the time and support they need, which means the number of places we can offer is limited and often tied to specific funding. Even so, we are happy to hear from people whose interests align closely with ours. We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds and are committed to building a diverse and inclusive group.

When you get in touch, please write to us in your own words. A short, genuine message telling us what drew you to our work and how it connects with your own interests means much more to us than a polished but generic email. Messages that read as AI-generated or as sent to many groups at once are hard for us to engage with, so a few personal lines really do go a long way.

## Undergraduate & MSc students

{{< opportunities category="undergraduate" >}}

We regularly supervise undergraduate and MSc students, both through dissertation projects and summer research projects. These are a great way to get a taste of research and to find out whether a PhD might be for you.

- **Dissertation projects.** If you are a Warwick student get in touch to discuss a project idea in our [research areas](/research/).
- **Summer research projects.** We are also happy to host summer students, typically funded through schemes such as the [Warwick Undergraduate Research Support Scheme (URSS)](https://warwick.ac.uk/services/skills/urss/) or external funding. Funding deadlines are usually early in the year, so contact us well in advance.

A strong background in applied mathematics (differential equations, numerical methods) and some programming experience, ideally in Python, are helpful, but curiosity and willingness to learn matter most.

## PhD students

{{< opportunities category="phd" >}}

Relevant PhD studentships are advertised here and through the usual university channels when available, but for a broader range of opportunities, we encourage candidates to check:

* [Mathematics Institute PhDs](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/studywithus/postgraduateresearch/).
* [CDT in Modelling of Heterogeneous Systems (HetSys)](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/hetsys/).

We are also happy to support strong candidates applying for competitive scholarships and doctoral training programmes.

Successful PhD candidates usually have a degree in mathematics, physics, engineering or a related discipline, with a solid grounding in differential equations, either analytically or numerically, and an interest in modelling real-world systems. Experience with scientific programming is an advantage.

If you are interested, we encourage you to get in touch before applying (many funding deadlines fall between December and February).

We are also open to hosting PhD students from other universities for short research visits, where there is a clear connection with our work. If you are thinking about a visit, please get in touch and tell us what you would like to work on and how it fits with your PhD project.

## Postdoctoral researchers

{{< opportunities category="postdoc" >}}

Postdoctoral positions funded by our research grants are advertised here and on the [Mathematics Institute vacancies page](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/maths/jobs/).

We also welcome enquiries from researchers interested in applying for independent fellowships to join the group, such as:

- [EPSRC Fellowships](https://www.ukri.org/apply-for-funding/our-fellowship-opportunities/), including the [Mathematical Sciences Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.ukri.org/opportunity/mathematical-sciences-postdoctoral-fellowship/)
- [Royal Commission for the Exhibition of 1851 Research Fellowships](https://royalcommission1851.org/fellowships/research-fellowships)
- [Leverhulme Trust Early Career Fellowships](https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/early-career-fellowships)
- [Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellowships](https://raeng.org.uk/programmes-and-prizes/programmes/uk-grants-and-prizes/support-for-research/research-fellowships/)
- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships)

Fellowship applications take time to prepare well and require departmental support, so please contact us at least three to six months before the deadline.

When you get in touch, please include your CV and a brief outline of the research you would like to pursue and how it connects to the work of the group.

## Collaborations and getting in touch

We also work closely with industrial and academic partners, from co-funded PhD studentships and student projects to joint research proposals and research visits. If you are interested in working together, we would be glad to hear from you.

To discuss any of these opportunities, please contact [Ferran Brosa Planella](/team/ferran-brosa-planella/).
