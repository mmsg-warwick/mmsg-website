At the Mathematical Modelling for Sustainability Group, we seek to advance sustainable energy technologies through mathematical modelling, computational methods, and open software tools. Alongside our main themes, we like to explore other areas where mathematical modelling can contribute to a more sustainable future.

## How we work

{{< methods >}}

{{< method title="Mathematical modelling" icon="modelling" >}}
Mathematical modelling runs through all of our work. We derive models from the underlying physics and use asymptotic analysis, homogenisation and numerical methods to simplify and analyse them, keeping a clear link between detailed and reduced models.
{{< /method >}}

{{< method title="Open-source software" icon="software" >}}
We implement our models in open-source software, so that others can reproduce, use and build on our work. We lead the development of some of these tools and contribute to others, with an emphasis on testing and documentation. See our [Software](/software/) page for details.
{{< /method >}}
