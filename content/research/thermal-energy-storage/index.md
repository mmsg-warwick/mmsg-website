## Overview

We develop mathematical models for thermal energy storage
technologies that can support the transition to more
sustainable energy systems.

Particular interest is placed on heat batteries and
technologies that enable flexible use of renewable energy.

## Research Questions

- How can thermal storage systems be modelled efficiently?
- What determines their long-term performance?
- How can thermal systems be optimised for real-world operation?

## Current Activities

- Heat batteries
- Thermal energy storage
- System optimisation
- Multiphysics modelling
