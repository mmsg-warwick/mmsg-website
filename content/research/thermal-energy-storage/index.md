Thermal energy storage, often called heat batteries, stores heat when energy is available and releases it when it is needed. We model latent heat storage, where energy is stored as a material melts and solidifies, and thermochemical storage, where it is stored in reversible chemical reactions.

Our models range from the processes within the storage material to the performance of complete systems, and aim to support the design and operation of practical devices.
