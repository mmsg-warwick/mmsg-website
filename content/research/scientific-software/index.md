## Overview

Open-source scientific software plays a fundamental role in
reproducible and impactful research.

We develop software tools that enable researchers and
engineers to build, analyse, and share mathematical models
for sustainable technologies.

## Research Questions

- How can scientific software be made more accessible?
- How can reproducibility be improved?
- How can research software support collaboration and education?
