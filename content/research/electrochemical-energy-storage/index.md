## Overview

We develop mathematical models and computational tools for
electrochemical energy storage systems, with a particular
focus on lithium-ion batteries.

Our work aims to improve understanding, design, control,
and optimisation of battery systems across multiple scales.

## Research Questions

- How can battery behaviour be predicted accurately and efficiently?
- How can models support battery management and control?
- How can degradation mechanisms be understood through modelling?
- How can simulation tools accelerate battery development?

## Current Activities

- Physics-based battery modelling
- Reduced-order models
- Battery degradation
- Numerical methods for battery simulation
- Battery management applications
